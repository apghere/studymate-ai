Cell division notes. Mitosis produces two genetically identical diploid daughter cells and is used for growth and repair. Its phases are prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase, followed by cytokinesis. Meiosis produces four genetically different haploid gametes and includes crossing over during prophase I.
